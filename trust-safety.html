Trust & safety

Keeping every delivery safe.

Trust is the whole product — here's how we protect both sides of every job.

Courier vetting

Every courier on the platform is ID-checked before they can accept jobs, and rated by customers after every delivery. Consistently low-rated couriers are removed.

Live tracking & proof

Every job includes live location tracking from collection to drop-off, plus a photo the moment it's delivered — so there's always a clear record.

Insurance

Deliveries booked through P Fanthoms are covered while in transit, giving you peace of mind on higher-value items.

Reporting a problem

If something goes wrong with a delivery, contact our support team via the contact page and we'll look into it.
