Ask Frank

Hey — I run P Fanthoms. Here's what people usually ask me before they book.

How does P Fanthoms work?
Tell us what's moving and where. We match you with a vetted local courier and give you an upfront price — no call needed.
Is this just for businesses?
Not at all — most bookings are individuals sending a parcel, selling on Marketplace, or moving furniture. Businesses are welcome too, with the same simple pricing.
How much does it cost?
It depends on distance, size and timing — you'll see the exact price before you book, and it won't change.
Can I track my delivery?
Yes — live location tracking from the moment your courier collects, plus a photo the moment it's delivered.
What areas do you cover?
UK-wide. Coverage is currently strongest across the North West, Midlands and Yorkshire, and growing daily.
How fast can something be delivered?
Same-day is available in most areas if booked before early afternoon. Next-day and scheduled options are always on offer too.
