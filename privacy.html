Privacy policy

This is a starter template — have it reviewed before relying on it for real bookings.

This policy explains what personal data P Fanthoms collects when you use the site, why, and what rights you have over it under UK GDPR.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, and role (customer or courier)
  • Booking details: pickup and drop-off addresses, item descriptions, price
  • Technical data: IP address and browser type, for security and fraud prevention

Why we collect it

To create your account, match your bookings with a courier, calculate accurate pricing based on distance, and let you track and manage your orders.

Who we share it with

Addresses and booking details are shared only with the courier assigned to your job, so they can complete the delivery. We use Supabase to store account and order data, and Ideal Postcodes to look up UK addresses — both process data on our behalf under their own data processing terms.

How long we keep it

Account and order data is kept for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and can object to how it's processed. Contact us via the contact page to exercise these rights.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies needed to keep you signed in and remember your session — no advertising or tracking cookies.
